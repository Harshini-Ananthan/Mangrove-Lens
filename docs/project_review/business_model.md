# Business Model Canvas: hypotheses, not traction

| Block | Honest current hypothesis |
|---|---|
| Customer segments | Coastal/forest departments and restoration-program operators; research/NGO analysts as users or pilot partners. No signed buyers. |
| Value proposition | Reviewable Sundarbans condition evidence from multiple sensors and a concise audit report; benefit over present workflow unmeasured. |
| Channels | Direct institutional pilot, university/agency collaboration, open demo and technical briefings. |
| Customer relationships | Co-designed analyst workflow, training, review feedback and support; no deployed support team. |
| Revenue streams | **Proposed** B2G/B2B annual hosted service or funded monitoring pilot; no price, contract or revenue validated. Open research access may coexist under separate terms. |
| Key activities | EO ingestion, quality control, scientific validation, model monitoring, reporting, user support, security. |
| Key resources | Earth Engine access, public Copernicus imagery, code/model, expert reviewers, independent labels, cloud compute. No proprietary imagery or exclusive data. |
| Key partners | **Prospective:** state forest/coastal agency, Bangladesh counterpart, NCSCM/FSI, university and field NGO; none documented as project partners. |
| Cost structure | Earth Engine/cloud/hosting, engineering, labelled imagery/field verification, security, training and support. Costs not quantified. |

**Model selection:** B2G is the primary *hypothesis*; B2B institutional and grant-funded research are alternatives. A CSR/NGO sponsor could fund a public-good deployment, but this is not a validated revenue stream. Google's current [Earth Engine commercial transition policy](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides/transition_to_commercial) (Google, accessed 12 Nov 2026) says operational government and commercial use may require a paid configuration; cost and eligibility must be included in a pilot quote. MISHTI is a government restoration initiative, not proof of procurement budget for this product (MoEFCC/PIB, **2025**, [source](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2159295&lang=2&reg=3)).
