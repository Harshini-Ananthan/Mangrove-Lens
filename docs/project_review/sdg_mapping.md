# SDG mapping: contribution pathways, not measured impact

| Goal and target | Plausible contribution if validated | Measurable project indicator | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| SDG 14.2: manage/protect/restore marine and coastal ecosystems | More timely, evidence-backed mangrove condition review | Confirmed incidents reviewed; median time from imagery to decision; verified restoration follow-up | **Proposed outcome**; current system only produces candidate alerts. UN DESA, *Goal 14*, [target text](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal14). |
| SDG 15.1: conservation and sustainable use of terrestrial/freshwater ecosystems | Support local forest management with documented observations | Area with verified monitoring coverage; action records linked to field evidence | **Proposed outcome**. UN DESA, *Goal 15*, [target text](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal15). |
| SDG 13.1: resilience to climate hazards | Supply decision support after storm/salinity stress, if users act on it | Time to triage post-event changes; proportion of reviews with field decision | **Indirect, proposed**; not resilience proof. UN DESA, *Goal 13*, [target text](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal13). |
| SDG 17.16/17.17: partnerships | Cross-border and research/agency data collaboration | Signed data/pilot agreements, joint validation exercises | **Not established**. UN DESA, *2030 Agenda*, **2015**, [target text](https://sdgs.un.org/2030agenda). |

Avoid carbon sequestration, avoided emissions, biodiversity gains, livelihoods or reduced disaster losses as **measured** results. The system currently measures satellite-derived condition proxies and creates reports; ecological/social outcomes need field and counterfactual evidence. The United Nations goal pages state target definitions, not endorsement of Mangrove Lens.
