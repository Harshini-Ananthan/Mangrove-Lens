# Market sizing: transparent opportunity units, no invented revenue

## Define the market narrowly

The first plausible market is **institutional mangrove monitoring workflows** around the Sundarbans. It is not all mangrove hectares, all climate software or the full MISHTI allocation. MoEFCC/PIB reports MISHTI activity across **13 Indian states/UTs** in 2023–25 ([press release, 21 Aug 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2159295&lang=2&reg=3)); this suggests at most 13 jurisdiction-level *prospects* within the India program, **not 13 buyers**. Bangladesh is an additional jurisdiction in the transboundary AOI (UNESCO, *The Sundarbans*, **2024**, [source](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/sundarbans?hub=70318)); its agency procurement structure is unverified here.

| Measure | Count / formula | Evidence and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| TAM, opportunity units | Up to **13 Indian jurisdiction prospects + Bangladesh institutional segment**; count agencies and budgets after interviews. | 13 is MISHTI activity geography, not interested accounts. Bangladesh is not necessarily one buyer. |
| SAM, near-term focus | **West Bengal + Bangladesh Sundarbans counterpart** (two geographic institutional segments). | Team's AOI and UNESCO geography; exact organizations, budgets and access unconfirmed. |
| SOM, achieved | **0 paying customers; 0 signed pilots documented.** Target a single controlled pilot as a hypothesis. | Repository contains no contract, user validation or procurement proof. |
| Monetary TAM/SAM/SOM | **Not estimable defensibly yet.** `eligible organizations × verified annual contract value`; both factors require buyer discovery and costed deployment. | No price, budget line, willingness-to-pay or sales cycle evidence. |

The **540 km²** MISHTI launch ambition (MoEFCC/PIB, **2023**, [source](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=1929939&lang=2&reg=48)) and West Bengal's **2,119.16 km²** mangrove cover (FSI, *ISFR 2023*, [source](https://fsi.nic.in/uploads/isfr2023/isfr_book_eng-vol-1_2023.pdf)) are ecological/program scales, not addressable revenue. No global dollar market figure is used. For review, present **opportunity units and the pricing research plan** rather than a fabricated ₹ TAM.

**To calculate a monetary market later:** identify budget owner and procurement categories; obtain 3–5 comparable tenders or public contracts; calculate fully loaded annual price (Earth Engine, cloud, field validation, support); confirm willingness to pay with at least two prospective buyers; bound uptake with sales-cycle and eligibility evidence. Until then, monetary sizing is an open question.
