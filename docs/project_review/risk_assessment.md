# Risk register with validation tests

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation | Validation gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cloud, tide, season and static mask create false canopy signals | An anomaly may reflect observation conditions, not degradation | Quality flags, tide-aware composites, updated mask, multi-date confirmation and human review | Blind incident set, precision/false alerts per month by season and tide. Mangrove mapping methodology: *Remote Sensing*, **2020**, [source](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/12/22/3758). |
| Single AOI average hides local events | Small clearing can disappear in a regional mean | Per-cell features/inference and uncertainty; keep current result labelled area-level | Inject/reference local event cases and measure detection versus GMW; current [dataset](../../reports/dataset_validation.json) has one region. |
| Model overfit/data leakage or drift | 78 modelling rows are small; one holdout insufficient | Rolling-origin tests, leakage checks, seasonal baselines, retrain review gate and drift monitoring | Pre-register holdouts and report MAE/precision by year/season; [evaluation](../../reports/model_evaluation.json). |
| CO misinterpreted as local cause | Sentinel-5P atmospheric CO is spatially coarse and non-causal | Keep as contextual only or remove from decision model if no incremental value | Ablation study and domain expert review; Google, *S5P OFFL CO* [catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S5P_OFFL_L3_CO). |
| Earth Engine outage, quota or commercial licence cost | Missed monthly run or unexpectedly unaffordable service | Retry, freshness alerts, quotas, cache and paid-plan costing | 90-day uptime/freshness log and billing estimate; Google, *Transition to commercial projects*, [policy](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides/transition_to_commercial). |
| Wrong official receives an unverified alert | Trust and reputational harm | Keep external dispatch disabled until recipient authorization, model approval and review workflow | Test SMTP in sandbox; explicit approval/dispatch audit; current [manifest](../../models/ndvi_forecast_rf.manifest.json) blocks approval. |
| No adoption or procurement | A useful demo may have no institutional owner | Discovery interviews, co-design, pilot with named budget owner | Interview notes, signed pilot, task completion and procurement pathway; current status in [user validation](user_validation.md). |
| Misstated ecological impact | NDVI cannot prove carbon, survival, biodiversity or avoided loss | Separate direct system metrics from field ecological outcomes | Matched field plots/verified interventions over time; McNulty et al., *Blue Carbon Explorer*, **2025**, [source](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/environmental-science/articles/10.3389/fenvs.2025.1641301/full). |

Priority order: independent event validation and spatial resolution first, then operator validation, then external notification and scale economics. A risk is considered mitigated only after its validation gate passes.
