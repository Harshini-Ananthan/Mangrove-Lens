# Indian government initiatives and relevance

| Initiative | Verified activity, scope and source | Relevance to Mangrove Lens |
|---|---|---|
| Mangrove Alliance for Climate (MAC) | India joined this Indonesia–UAE-led international coalition at COP27. MoEFCC via Press Information Bureau (PIB), *Union Minister speaks at launch of MAC*, **8 Nov 2022**, [source](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1874502&lang=2&reg=48). | Policy and knowledge-sharing alliance; not evidence that India lacks monitoring. Do not confuse MAC with the Global Mangrove Alliance or its Global Mangrove Watch platform. |
| MISHTI | MoEFCC launched Mangrove Initiative for Shoreline Habitats & Tangible Incomes on **5 June 2023**, with an announced **540 km²** restoration ambition over five years; MoEFCC/PIB, *MISHTI launched*, **2023**, [source](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=1929939&lang=2&reg=48). A later MoEFCC/PIB release states **22,560.34 ha** covered in 2023–25 across 13 states/UTs, **21 Aug 2025**, [source](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2159295&lang=2&reg=3). | Restoration program and potential evaluation partner; it already includes monitoring/evaluation. Reported area is program coverage, not necessarily independently measured survival or a software budget. |
| Forest Survey of India (FSI) | Biennial national mangrove inventory in *India State of Forest Report 2023*, FSI/MoEFCC, **2023**, [vol. 1](https://fsi.nic.in/uploads/isfr2023/isfr_book_eng-vol-1_2023.pdf). India 4,991.68 km²; West Bengal 2,119.16 km². | Official baseline. Our monthly AOI NDVI cannot replace the inventory, which measures extent through an established mapping method. Reconcile area and class definitions before comparing. |
| National Centre for Sustainable Coastal Management (NCSCM) | *Mangrove Community Zonation Atlas of India*, NCSCM/MoEFCC, released **4 Sept 2024**, maps community zonation using 2016–19 Landsat 8 data for nine coastal states and four UTs, [atlas page](https://ncscm.res.in/mza-2/). NCSCM also publishes a [mangrove forest structure protocol](https://ncscm.res.in/guidelines-manuals/) (date not stated). | Existing science partner or comparator. Atlas is species/community context and historical baseline, not a live anomaly service. Avoid saying NCSCM has no decision-support capability. |
| National Coastal Mission | MoEFCC describes assistance for conservation/management of **38 identified mangrove sites** and four coral reef sites across coastal states/UTs; PIB, written parliamentary response, **2025**, [source](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2115836&lang=2&reg=48). | Possible institutional route to validation and procurement; no direct demand has been established. |

## Policy implications

Propose a **pilot with a state forest/coastal agency**, using FSI and NCSCM products as references and MISHTI monitoring needs as a use case. Procurement, data-sharing, field-access and reporting requirements need interviews. A policy initiative is not a customer commitment. India–Bangladesh data sharing adds a separate partner and governance track. Publicly available satellite data can cover both sides, but no bilateral operational agreement has been documented here.
