## Research implications

Mangrove mapping from optical imagery is sensitive to tidal stage and mixed land/water edges; evaluate tide-conditioned composites before attributing a change to canopy loss. Yancho et al., *GEEMMM*, **2020**, [study](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/12/22/3758), explicitly implements tidal calibration. Restoration monitoring cannot establish survival, biodiversity or soil carbon from NDVI alone; McNulty et al. explicitly discuss the need for field measurements for carbon inference, [2025 article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/environmental-science/articles/10.3389/fenvs.2025.1641301/full). A forecast residual is a **screening statistic**, not a causal environmental diagnosis.

Risk prediction is **not implemented** as a calibrated future loss probability. The Random Forest predicts current-month AOI mean NDVI using prior months. To claim risk prediction, train on independent future loss/impact labels and evaluate temporal and spatial generalization against no-change, seasonal and GMW baselines.
